[상세 내용] (초록 4개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Epithelial surfaces are the primary sites of lytic herpes simplex virus 1 (HSV-1) infection following initial exposure or reactivation from latency.
  · Our data suggest delayed progression of the HSV-1 life cycle in epidermal keratinocytes and hair follicle cells compared to the dermis.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · To model this complex environment, we used human hair-bearing skin organoids and combined single-cell spatial transcriptomics with imaging to map viral spread and host responses across skin architecture.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Infection triggered spatially organized host responses, including induction of IER3 in papillary fibroblasts.
  · In basal keratinocytes, IRF3 was up-regulated in both infected and neighboring uninfected cells, consistent with paracrine activation of antiviral signaling.
  · Inflammatory responses were compartmentalized in a cell type-specific manner, with TNFSF9 (4-1BBL) induced in papillary fibroblasts and tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α) induced in keratinocytes.
  · Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assays confirmed secretion of these cytokines and the TNF-α-responsive chemokines CXCL2 and CXCL10.
  · CXCL2 expression was enriched in cells adjacent to TNF-α-expressing cells, highlighting spatial coordination of inflammatory signaling.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion (자동 구분)
  · While interferon-β reduced viral replication, TNF-α and 4-1BBL did not, suggesting that their primary role is to orchestrate immune cell recruitment and activation.
