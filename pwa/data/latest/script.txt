◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Mechanical stress on the pilosebaceous unit can contribute to mechanical alopecia (MA) as seen with traction alopecia (TA) and trichotillomania (TTM).
  · While some histological features that point to TA and TTM have been described, these may be absent in biopsies obtained at earlier stages of disease.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · A blinded, retrospective, qualitative, and semiquantitative study was performed comparing horizontally processed scalp biopsy sections of clinicopathologically confirmed TA (n = 16) and TTM (n = 9) to nearly matched controls (n = 15).
  · Further validation studies in larger cohorts are warranted to assess their diagnostic utility in the recognition of MA.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Additional anomalies of the pilosebaceous unit have been observed that may assist in the histopathological detection of MA.
  · Translocation of sebocytes into the follicular canal was seen in both TA and TTM, supporting a shared underlying mechanism.
  · Vectorially warped sebaceous lobules were observed predominately in TA (p = 0.0002), while collapse of the inner root sheath of the lower follicular isthmus and surface lichenification were primarily seen in TTM (p < 0.0002).
  · Lateral impingement of the follicular canal was seen in both TA and TTM (p < 0.0001), although more commonly in TA.
  · These alterations were observed in the absence of other previously described features.
