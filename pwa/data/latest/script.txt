◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Emerging non-invasive treatments include laser-assisted delivery of biomimetic growth factor-based serums.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · The aim of the study was to evaluate the safety and efficacy of non-ablative fractional laser (NAFL)-assisted delivery, with or without adjunct red light therapy, in patients with AGA.
  · In this pilot study, patients were randomly assigned to: (I) NAFL-assisted serum delivery plus red light therapy (triple), (II) NAFL-assisted serum delivery alone (dual), or (III) NAFL plus red light therapy without serum (EBD-only).

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Androgenetic alopecia (AGA) is the most common form of hair loss and can significantly impact quality of life.
  · Primary outcomes were hair density and thickness.
  · Secondary outcomes included patient satisfaction, tolerability, and adverse events.
  · Hair density increased in all groups, with greater improvement in the triple (25.6 ± 7.0 %) and dual (22.7 ± 3.4 %) groups compared to EBD-only (4.9 ± 9.2 %).
  · Hair thickness improved in both serum groups but not in the EBD-only group.
  · Clinical outcomes were similar between triple and dual treatments, though satisfaction was higher with triple therapy.
