◆ 그 밖의 내용 — Purpose Of Review
  · Persistent chemotherapy-induced alopecia (pCIA) is defined as incomplete hair growth ≥6 months after chemotherapy completion.
  · This scoping review aimed to identify gaps for future research by mapping pCIA's incidence, risk factors, quality-of-life (QoL) impacts, presentation, prevention, and management.

◆ 주요 결과 — Findings
  · Thirty-nine articles were included.
  · The most common study type was case reports (12/39, 30.8%).
  · Only 2/39 (5.1%) were randomized controlled trials (RCTs).
  · Most studies (27/39, 69.2%) reported pCIA in patients with breast cancer.
  · Alkylating agents, taxanes, and anthracyclines were most frequently reported to be associated with pCIA.
  · All six studies that evaluated QoL reported that pCIA negatively affected patients' psychological, functional, and social well-being.
