◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · At 6 months, 39 patients (54.2%) responded.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · We retrospectively analyzed 72 intravenous methylprednisolone (IVMP)-treated patients with moderate to severe alopecia areata to assess predictors of ≥ 50% improvement in the Severity of Alopecia Tool score at 6 months, the predictive value of 3-month hair regrowth and exploratory long-term outcomes.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Shorter disease duration was associated with response (OR: per 1-unit increase in ln(months): 0.69, 95% CI: 0.51-0.95; p = 0.021), as was treatment within 6 months (OR: 4.89, 95% CI: 1.76-13.63; p = 0.002).
  · After excluding possible self-healing acute diffuse and total alopecia cases, the adjusted association between disease duration and 6-month treatment response was no longer significant (OR: 0.74, 95% CI: 0.54-1.02; p = 0.063), whereas treatment initiation within 6 months of disease onset remained significantly associated with response.
  · Among 46 patients with 3-month data, hair regrowth predicted the 6-month response (AUC: 0.872); a 20% threshold yielded 100% specificity and 70.8% sensitivity.
  · Exploratory long-term outcomes were more favorable with shorter disease duration.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion (자동 구분)
  · Three-month hair regrowth may provide an early quantitative indicator of IVMP response, while the independent continuous association between disease duration and outcome warrants further investigation.
