◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Tissue engineering holds substantial potential for constructing normal skin with appendages such as hair follicles, directly restoring skin's complete physiological functions.
  · The current challenge lies in recreating biomimetic hair follicle organs with high-level cell density and architecture.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · Here, we developed an acoustofluidic-assisted 3D bioprinting platform to fabricate bionic skins with efficient hair follicle regeneration capability.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Pre-formed hair follicle germs, derived from co-cultured epidermal and dermal mesenchymal stem cells, were encapsulated within a hydrogel bioink.
  · Controlled acoustic fields generated flow vortices that directed cell migration and promoted organized cellular assembly from initially disordered states.
  · Guided cell aggregates were then patterned through bioprinting to construct a grid-structured bionic skin.
  · This architecture provided stable 3D support while maintaining high porosity, enabling efficient gas exchange.
  · The printed bionic skin exhibited well-organized spatial cell distribution, high cellular viability, and promising differentiation potential.
  · Further in vivo experiments demonstrated the efficiency of the bionic skin in accelerating mature hair regeneration, showing promise for clinical alopecia treatment.
