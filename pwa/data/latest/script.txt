◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · A hair transplant may restore advanced androgenetic alopecia or scalp scars.
  · Suitable patients have sufficient donor hair, at least for their current stage of alopecia.
  · The planning of the hair line, graft number, and hair distribution should be sustainable as the alopecia may progress and the donor hair is limited.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · The procedure is performed in local anaesthesia and consists of several manual steps, using microsurgical instruments and loupe magnification.
  · In most cases, each follicular unit containing 1-4 hairs is harvested individually using special micropunches (follicular unit excision, FUE).

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Hair-preserving medications are therefore recommended.
  · Digital trichoscopy and artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted hair measurements may aid in the assessment of the recipient and donor area and help to exclude inflammatory hair and scalp conditions.
  · Each skin cut should be performed by the operating hair surgeon.
  · This includes the nontraumatic excision of the follicular units and the creation of recipient sites in a natural direction, followed by a careful graft insertion.
