[상세 내용] (초록 4개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Purpose
  · This study aimed to evaluate retinal microvascular and structural changes in patients with alopecia areata (AA) using optical coherence tomography angiography (OCTA).
  · To our knowledge, retinal microvascular alterations have not been investigated via OCTA in AA before.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · This prospective study included 40 patients with newly diagnosed AA and 42 healthy controls.
  · Superficial capillary plexus vessel densities (VD-SCP) and deep capillary plexus vessel densities (VD-DCP), choriocapillaris flow area, foveal avascular zone (FAZ), retinal nerve fiber layer (RNFL) thickness, optic disc vascular parameters, and central corneal thickness (CCT) were measured.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · VD-SCP was significantly reduced in the whole image (p = 0.001), parafoveal (p = 0.001), and perifoveal regions (p = 0.007) in patients with AA compared with controls.
  · These differences remained significant after FDR correction (adjusted p = 0.011, 0.011, and 0.049, respectively).
  · However, foveal VD-SCP did not differ significantly (p = 0.221).
  · An unadjusted difference in inferior RNFL thickness (p = 0.044) did not remain statistically significant after FDR correction (adjusted p = 0.231), whereas global RNFL thickness showed no significant difference.
  · No significant differences were observed between groups in VD-DCP, FAZ area, choriocapillaris flow, optic disc vascular parameters, foveal thickness, or CCT (all p > 0.05).

◆ 결론 — Conclusion
  · Patients with AA demonstrate reduced VD-SCP except in the foveal region, without evidence of widespread retinal or choroidal vascular impairment.
  · These findings suggest that AA may be associated with early subclinical alterations in retinal microcirculation.
  · OCTA may serve as a useful non-invasive tool for detecting subtle ocular microvascular changes in patients with AA.
