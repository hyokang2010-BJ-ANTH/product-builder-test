◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Hair transplantation (HT) has become an important reconstructive option for selected patients with stable cicatricial alopecia.
  · However, objective methods for assessing recipient-site suitability before surgery remain limited.
  · The objective of the study is to describe the structural and vascular ultrasound findings in patients with cicatricial alopecia undergoing preoperative evaluation for HT.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · This retrospective observational study included 22 patients with cicatricial alopecia evaluated with high-frequency ultrasound (HFUS) and color Doppler between 2014 and 2025.
  · Structural variables included dermal and subcutaneous thickness, follicular characteristics, and follicular diameter, while vascular assessment included Doppler patterns and peak systolic velocity.
  · HFUS consistently demonstrated dermal thinning, reduced or absent subcutaneous adipose tissue, and decreased or absent follicular structures.
  · Eleven patients underwent HT, of whom 4 required recipient-site optimization with autologous fat grafting before follicular unit excision.
  · All 4 demonstrated preoperative dermal thickness <3 mm and reduced or absent subcutaneous adipose tissue on ultrasound.
  · At 12 months, graft survival exceeded 90% in 8 patients and was approximately 80% in 3 patients.
