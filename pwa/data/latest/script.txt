[상세 내용] (초록 3개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Aging leads to significant hair changes, including alopecia and graying, yet the underlying mechanisms remain unclear.
  · Emerging evidence highlights the role of the extracellular microenvironment, particularly dermal white adipose tissue (dWAT), in regulating hair regeneration and pigmentation.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · This study investigates key molecular mechanisms in dWAT governing hair aging, aiming to inform targeted therapies for age-related hair disorders.
  · Using in vivo murine models, protein arrays, FACS, immunofluorescence, and transcriptomics, we identified monocyte chemoattractant protein-1 (MCP-1) as a critical factor secreted by aged dWAT.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Previous studies showed that young murine dWAT improves age-related hair phenotypes via follistatin upregulation.
  · MCP-1 recruits macrophages to perifollicular regions.
  · Depleting skin macrophages in aged mice shifted hair follicles from telogen to anagen and upregulated melanocyte-lineage proteins, including DCT, Tyrosinase-related protein 1 (TYRP1), and Pmel17.
  · Transcriptomic analysis revealed macrophage absence activated signaling pathways essential for hair growth and melanogenesis, upregulating hair follicle development genes.
  · Conversely, M0 and M2 macrophages were associated with earlier anagen entry, whereas M1 macrophages delayed anagen transition.
