[상세 내용] (초록 5개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background
  · Studies of alopecia areata-associated white hair (AA-WH) are scarce.

◆ 배경 — Objectives
  · To stratify and evaluate the pathophysiological behaviour of AA-WH.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · This large-scale retrospective study used clinical data, dermatoscopic findings and histopathological examination.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · AA-WH was identified in 16% (n = 826/5155) of patients with AA.
  · Incidence increased with grey hair and age, up to 44.4% (n = 79/178) in patients ≥55 years old.
  · Risk factors for AA-WH included older onset age, higher Severity of Alopecia Tool (SALT) scores, comorbidity with vitiligo, abnormality in thyroid function/autoantibodies and positive antinuclear antibody (ANA) tests.
  · Compared with patients without AA-WH (AA-NWH), patients with AA-WH were older in age (P < 0.05) and in age of onset of AA (P < 0.05).
  · Clinical AA-WH patterns could be mosaic or uniformly white; localized, diffuse and hairline distribution patterns were identified.
  · Mosaic presentations predominated across all AA clinical subtypes, except for alopecia totalis/universalis (AT/AU).

◆ 결론 — Conclusions
  · AA-WH correlates with ageing, grey hair and autoimmunity across all age groups and allergy in juveniles.
  · Hairline AA-WH correlates with elevated T helper 2-cytokine activity.
  · Depigmentation of hair roots and growth of white vellus hairs contribute to AA-WH.
  · Both depigmentation and repigmentation of hairs were observed without evidence of follicular cycling.
