◆ 배경 — Background
  · Alopecia areata (AA) is an autoimmune disorder characterized by non-scarring hair loss.
  · Although baricitinib is effective in severe AA, biomarkers associated with subsequent treatment response remain unclear.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · In this retrospective real-world study, 110 Chinese patients with severe AA received baricitinib for up to 36 weeks.
  · Baseline plasma samples from 51 patients with AA and 22 healthy controls underwent proteomic profiling.
  · Proteins associated with Week 24 achievement of SALT20 (absolute SALT score ≤ 20) or SALT50 (≥ 50% reduction from baseline) were evaluated alongside clinical variables in multivariable analyses.
  · Selected candidate biomarkers were further evaluated by ELISA.
  · Paired baseline and Week 24 samples were analyzed in 19 patients.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · At Week 24, 38.2% of patients achieved SALT20 and 54.5% achieved SALT50; by Week 36, these rates increased to 49.3% and 60.3%, respectively.
  · Week 24 achievement of SALT20 or SALT50 was associated with shorter disease duration, lower baseline SALT score, and lower frequencies of AU/AT and eyebrow/eyelash involvement.
  · Compared with healthy controls, patients with AA showed distinct baseline plasma proteomic alterations.
  · Achievers showed a relatively higher baseline inflammatory plasma proteomic profile, and exploratory longitudinal analyses revealed broader post-treatment reductions in inflammatory proteins.
  · CCL11 was higher at baseline in patients who subsequently achieved SALT20 or SALT50 and remained associated with both endpoints in multivariable analysis.

◆ 결론 — Conclusions
  · Baricitinib response was heterogeneous in patients with severe AA.
  · Better outcomes were associated with higher baseline levels of several inflammatory plasma proteins.
  · CCL11 emerged as a candidate response-associated biomarker.
