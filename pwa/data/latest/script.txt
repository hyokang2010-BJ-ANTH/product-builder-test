◆ 배경 — Background
  · Janus kinase inhibitors have reshaped the management of severe alopecia areata, but evidence for intra-class switching after an inadequate or non-durable benefit from baricitinib remains limited.

◆ 배경 — Objective
  · We aimed to assess real-world effectiveness after switching from baricitinib to ritlecitinib 50 mg once daily and to explore baseline factors associated with the post-switch response.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · We performed a multicentre retrospective cohort study across Italian referral centres.
  · Eligible patients had alopecia areata, had received baricitinib for at least 24 weeks and were switched to ritlecitinib 50 mg once daily.
  · All switched patients with post-switch outcomes were included irrespective of prior baricitinib response status.
  · The primary endpoint was a Severity of Alopecia Tool (SALT) score ≤ 20 at any post-switch assessment.
  · Secondary endpoints were longitudinal SALT change and time to the first post-switch SALT score ≤ 20.
  · Exploratory logistic and Cox models included age, current alopecia areata episode duration, SALT at ritlecitinib initiation (switch) and prior SALT50 response to baricitinib.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · Overall, 152 patients underwent a treatment switch and 138 had a post-switch follow-up.
  · During baricitinib, a SALT50 score was recorded in 10/141 patients (7.1%) and a SALT score ≤ 20 at the final baricitinib assessment in 5/144 (3.5%); prior response was not an exclusion criterion.
  · During ritlecitinib follow-up, 30/138 patients (21.7%) had a SALT score ≤ 20.
  · Median SALT score changed from 95 at ritlecitinib initiation to 70 at week 24 and 10 at week 52 among patients observed at those visits, but a late follow-up was sparse.
  · No baseline covariate showed clear evidence of association with the overall binary endpoint.
  · In the exploratory Cox model, a higher SALT score at ritlecitinib initiation was modestly associated with slower achievement of a SALT score ≤ 20 (hazard ratio 0.98, 95% confidence interval 0.95-1.00; P = 0.034), with the confidence interval approaching the null.

◆ 결론 — Conclusions
  · In this predominantly baricitinib-refractory, clinically switched cohort, a SALT score ≤ 20 was observed during the ritlecitinib follow-up in about one fifth of evaluable patients.
  · The association between greater severity at ritlecitinib initiation and slower response was modest and should not be considered a validated predictor.
  · Retrospective treatment selection, prior baricitinib responses in a small subgroup, late attrition, and unmeasured washout or concomitant-treatment effects limit treatment-specific attribution.
  · These findings suggest that ritlecitinib may be a salvage option for selected patients and warrant prospective sequencing studies.
