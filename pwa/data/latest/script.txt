[상세 내용] (초록 5개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background
  · Follicular unit excision (FUE) has become predominant in hair restoration, yet precise recipient-site creation depends on accurate graft length.
  · Follicular unit length (FUL) influences recipient-site depth, and mismatches may cause graft protrusion, dehydration, or folliculitis.
  · The extent to which FUL varies predictably across donor regions within patients, enabling region-specific planning, remains poorly characterized.

◆ 배경 — Objective
  · The aim of this study was to characterize FUL variation across 14 donor scalp areas and establish a reference value in an admixed Brazilian population.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · This cross-sectional observational study measured intraoperative FUL in 98 men undergoing first-time FUE at a single Brazilian center (May 2018-July 2019).
  · In each of 14 scalp areas, 10 intact grafts per participant were measured in consensus by 2 surgeons.
  · Participant-level means were computed per area and overall; their mean defined the universal follicular length (UFL).
  · Regional differences were analyzed using a linear mixed-effects model.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · Among 13,720 grafts, UFL was 4.68 (SD, 0.13) mm.
  · The mean FUL ranged from 4.43 mm (inferior temporal) to 4.89 mm (central superior occipital; difference, 0.46 mm; 10.4%).
  · Age, weight, height, body mass index, and Norwood-Hamilton classification were not significantly associated with FUL.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion
  · Follicular unit length varies significantly by donor scalp area.
  · Region-informed planning and direct intraoperative measurement support precision-based FUE.
