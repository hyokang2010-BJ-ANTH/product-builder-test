[한눈에 보기]
- This cross-sectional survey study explores the experience, comfort and knowledge of dermatology residents and attendings in managing testosterone-induced acne (TiAV) and androgenic alopecia (TiAGA) in trans men.
- While most respondents reported some level of comfort treating these conditions, objective knowledge scores were modest and revealed gaps across both conditions, particularly TiAGA.
- Our findings suggest that current LGBTQIA+-focused education may be perceived as adequate but may not translate into knowledge retention or clinical preparedness.
