제목: Study on Construction of and Pharmacodynamics of Baicalin-Minoxidil Co-Loaded Nanogel
저널: Pharmaceutics
저자: Chen L, Liu E, Liu X 외
발행: 2026 Oct 15
원문: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42942839/

[한눈에 보기]
- Androgenetic alopecia (AGA) is a chronic and highly prevalent hair loss disorder for which current topical therapies are limited by undesirable side effects, dose-dependent efficacy, and poor skin penetration.
- Objective: Here, we developed a baicalin-minoxidil co-loaded nanogel (Bai-Min-NGs) to synergistically enhance transdermal drug delivery and promote hair regeneration.
- Methods: The optimized nanogel exhibited favorable colloidal attributes, featuring a nanoscale hydrodynamic diameter of 156 ± 3.62 nm, narrow size distribution (polydispersity index of 0.144 ± 0.011), surface zeta potential of -26.1 ± 0.37 mV, high encapsulation efficiencies (95.86 ± 1.34% for baicalin and 82.16 ± 2.50% for minoxidil), and satisfactory drug loading capacities (1.92 ± 0.08% and 1.64 ± 0.06%, respectively).

[상세 내용] (논문 전문 5개 섹션에서 발췌)

◆ 배경 — 1. Introduction
  · Androgenetic alopecia (AGA) is a pervasive dermatological condition affecting hundreds of millions of people worldwide, characterized by progressive follicular miniaturization and disrupted hair growth cycles.
  · Despite the availability of various therapeutic modalities, minoxidil (Min), the first-line clinical drug, still faces formidable challenges in practical application.
  · On one hand, commercially available formulations typically contain high concentrations of ethanol and propylene glycol, which frequently lead to severe allergic contact dermatitis and scalp dryness upon long-term use.

◆ 연구 방법 — 2. Materials and Methods
  · Cells were cultured in the medium supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS) and 1% penicillin-streptomycin at 37 °C in a humidified atmosphere containing 5% CO2.
  · After treatment, cells were incubated with 50 μM LysoTracker Red DND-99 for 90 min to label lysosomes, followed by fixation with 4% paraformaldehyde for 15 min.

◆ 주요 결과 — 3. Results
  · The measurement results demonstrated that Bai-Min-NEs possessed an average hydrodynamic diameter of 156 ± 3.62 nm, and the size distribution curve exhibited a symmetrical unimodal distribution, indicating a homogeneous dispersion of the system.
  · Regarding the drug-loading performance, the EE of Bai-Min-NEs for baicalin and minoxidil reached 95.863 ±1.338% and 82.158 ± 2.495%, respectively, which is beneficial for the subsequent drug delivery into the deep layers of the skin.
  · As shown in Figure 2A, the crude baicalin spectrum exhibited representative characteristic absorption peaks at 3490 cm−1, 3390 cm−1, 1661 cm−1 and 1609 cm−1, corresponding to intermolecular hydroxyl groups, free hydroxyl groups, C=C double bonds, and benzene ring C-H stretching vibrations, respectively.
  · The spectrum of minoxidil showed characteristic absorption peaks at 3411 cm−1 (-NH2), 1644 cm−1 (C=C) and 1555 cm−1 (C=N).
  · Furthermore, Korsmeyer–Peppas fitting yielded release exponents (n) of 1.2429 (R2 = 0.9200) for minoxidil and 1.4553 (R2 = 0.9875) for baicalin.
  · The combination index (CI) values across all tested concentrations remained well below 1.0 (ranging from 0.100 to 0.405), reaching 0.271 at the optimal concentration of 5.0 μg/mL.
  · (* p < 0.05, ** p < 0.01, *** p < 0.001, and **** p < 0.0001 (vs. model group); # p < 0.05 and ## p < 0.01 (vs. normal group)).

◆ 고찰 — 4. Discussion
  · The prepared Bai-Min-NEs exhibited a uniform size distribution (droplet diameter < 200 nm) and stable negative zeta potential.
  · Sub-micron particles are known to enhance skin penetration via intercellular and follicular routes, while surfactants within the nanoemulsion system disrupt stratum corneum lipid bilayers to improve transdermal delivery.
  · Although the negative surface charge limits non-specific adsorption, it supports colloidal stability and particle diffusion within the skin.
  · Specifically, the mathematical fitting confirmed a Super Case-II transport mechanism (n > 0.89), verifying that the swelling-controlled matrix relaxation guarantees constant zero-order release kinetics, thereby preventing rapid local drug depletion.

◆ 결론 — 5. Conclusions
  · In summary, this study successfully developed a novel dual-drug-loaded nanogel delivery system: Bai-Min-NGs, providing a highly promising and innovative strategy to overcome the bottlenecks of poor transdermal permeability and low bioavailability associated with traditional topical treatments for AGA.
  · Leveraging the three-dimensional synergistic effect between the high-pressure homogenized nanoemulsion core and the gel network, Bai-Min-NGs effectively overcome the robust physical barrier of the stratum corneum.
  · This system not only achieves prolonged retention and controlled release of Baicalin and Minoxidil within the deep layers of the skin but also targets DPCs via efficient endocytic pathways.
