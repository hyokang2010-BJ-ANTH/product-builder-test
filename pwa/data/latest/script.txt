[상세 내용] (초록 3개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Bone morphogenetic protein 4 (BMP4) has been implicated in hair follicle telogen maintenance, but its downstream mechanisms in human hair follicle cells remain incompletely understood.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · We examined whether BMP4 regulates fibroblast growth factor 18 (FGF18) through FOXP1 in dermal papilla-epithelial signaling.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · In immortalized human dermal papilla cells (iDPCs), cyclosporin A (CsA) decreased BMP4 expression and increased Noggin expression, and the NFAT inhibitor VIVIT produced similar effects.
  · CsA also reduced BMP4 protein levels in culture supernatants.
  · In immortalized human outer root sheath cells (iORSCs), BMP4 increased FGF18 and FOXP1 expression, with significant responses at concentrations as low as 0.1 ng/mL.
  · Noggin and the BMP type I receptor inhibitor LDN193189 suppressed these responses.
  · BMP4 induced Smad1 phosphorylation and increased FOXP1 protein levels, whereas FOXP1 knockdown prevented BMP4-induced FGF18 upregulation.
  · In primary human DPCs, CsA decreased BMP4 expression, and in primary human ORSCs, BMP4 increased FGF18 expression.
  · In Transwell co-culture, CsA pretreatment or BMP4 neutralization attenuated DPC-induced FGF18 and FOXP1 expression, whereas recombinant BMP4 significantly restored their expression.
