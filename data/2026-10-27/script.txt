◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Both poliosis (circumscribed depigmentation of the hair in any area of the body) and halo nevi (a melanocytic nevus which acquires a surrounding depigmented rim) are characterized by depigmentation driven by a T-cell-mediated loss of functional melanocytes within the epidermis and hair bulbs.
  · Halo nevi and poliosis are a relatively frequent phenomenon occurring in 1% of the children and adolescents with an average age of onset of 15 years and has no gender predisposition.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Both can be associated with vitiligo and are occasionally seen together as the same cytotoxic immune response (specifically CD8+ T cells) that destroys nevus melanocytes which can attack the same melanocyte-specific targets surrounding epidermal and follicular melanocytes.
  · While poliosis is frequently seen on the scalp, halo nevi on the scalp have been scarcely reported in the literature.
