◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Alopecia areata (AA) is an immune-mediated, nonscarring hair disorder with collapse of follicular immune privilege.
  · Diagnosis is based on clinical examination and trichoscopy.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Preserved follicular ostia distinguish it from scarring alopecias.
  · The Severity of Alopecia Tool (SALT) score quantifies scalp hair loss, but not activity or burden-both require separate assessment.
  · JAK inhibitors are the first approved systemic therapies.
  · Response follows the hair cycle and can only be judged after 3-6 months.
  · The following apply for children and adolescents: separate algorithms, evidence and approvals are limited, and families should be included in care.
