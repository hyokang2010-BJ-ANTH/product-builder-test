[상세 내용] (초록 4개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Androgenetic alopecia (AGA) is a common hair loss disorder caused by genetic susceptibility and androgen-driven follicular miniaturization.
  · Current FDA-approved therapies, including finasteride and minoxidil, are limited by adverse effects.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods (자동 구분)
  · Here, we developed a hyaluronic acid-based liposomal transdermal delivery system loaded with L-arginine (HL@L-Arg) to enhance cutaneous delivery and promote endogenous nitric oxide (NO) production through nitric oxide synthase.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Transcriptomic analysis indicated significant changes in pathways related to inflammatory responses, Wnt signaling, and steroid hormone biosynthesis.
  · Mechanistically, HL@L-Arg inhibited 5α-reductase activity and reduced dihydrotestosterone (DHT) production while promoting endogenous NO generation.
  · In an AGA mouse model, HL@L-Arg reduced TNF-α, IL-6, and androgen receptor (AR) expression, increased VEGF and Ki67 levels, and markedly promoted hair regeneration.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion (자동 구분)
  · Collectively, transdermal L-Arg delivery represents a promising endogenous NO-based therapeutic strategy for AGA through DHT suppression, regulation of the follicular microenvironment, and anti-inflammatory activity.
