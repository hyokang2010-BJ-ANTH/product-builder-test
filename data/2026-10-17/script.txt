◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Introduction: Current treatments for androgenetic alopecia (AGA) primarily suppress androgen signaling or stimulate hair growth.
  · Recent single-cell and spatial transcriptomic studies identify fibroimmune remodeling as a potential source of new AGA targets.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Areas covered: This review integrates transcriptomic and mechanistic evidence linking androgen-responsive fibroblasts, immune-cell recruitment, and extracellular matrix remodeling, in male and female pattern hair loss.
  · Herein, the authors assessed published human scalp, cell and animal studies of JAK/STAT, TGF-β, CXCL12-CXCR4/ACKR3, and DKK3-CKAP4, and evaluated RNA therapeutic modalities and delivery barriers.
  · Literature was identified primarily through PubMed in the last 20 years.
  · Expert opinion: Fibroimmune remodeling should be viewed as a complementary, rather than alternative, framework to androgen biology.
  · The most promising drug discovery strategy is to identify molecularly defined patient subsets and locally inhibit nodal mediators that couple fibroblast activation to inflammation and matrix remodeling.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion (자동 구분)
  · RNA therapeutics are well suited to otherwise difficult-to-drug intracellular targets, but progress will require human spatial validation, rigorous target prioritization, reproducible delivery, and pharmacodynamic biomarkers demonstrating restoration of the regenerative follicular niche.
