제목: Spruce-derived extracellular vesicles (SDEVs) improve hair loss by activating the proliferation of dermal papilla cells
저널: PloS one
저자: Zhang K, Cao Y, Wang Z 외
발행: 2026
원문: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42993777/

[한눈에 보기]
- Hair loss is a global health problem, and current conventional treatments, despite their effectiveness in delaying hair loss and improving hair growth, suffer from significant side effects and limitations in individualized treatment.
- In recent years, the application of novel nanotechnology in dermatology and regenerative medicine has gained attention.
- In this context, researchers explored the potential of applying Spruce-Derived Extracellular Vesicles (SDEVs) to hair loss intervention.

[상세 내용] (논문 전문 4개 섹션에서 발췌)

◆ 배경 — 1. Introduction
  · Hair loss is a common dermatological and cosmetic concern that can susbstantially affect quality of life.With the development of science and technology, modern medicine has made significant progress in addressing hair loss, mainly through medication and surgery, but these methods either have side effects or require long-term adherence and are costly.
  · Therefore, increasing attention has been directed toward alternative or adjunctive approaches.
  · In this context, plants and their extracts show great potential for use in hair loss prevention and treatment due to their high safety, low side effects, and significant efficacy.

◆ 연구 방법 — 2. Materials and methods
  · The plant residue was discarded and the filtrate was collected, transferred to a 50 mL centrifuge tube and centrifuged at 2000 × g for 15 min at 4 °C, and the supernatant was collected and centrifuged at 120,000 × g for 90 min at 4°C.
  · Samples were lysed in BPP buffer containing 100 mM EDTA, 50 mM borax, 50 mM vitamin C, 30% sucrose, 100 mM Tris base, 1% Triton X-100, 1%−5% PVPP, and 5 mM DTT, and protease inhibitors, followed by grinding at −40 °C for four cycles.
  · Peptides were separated on an AUR3-15075 C18 analytical column at 50 °C and 300 nL/ min using a 30 min gradient of 2.2%−44% solvent B over 21 min, followed by 44%−90% over 3 min, holding at 90% for 3 min, and re-equilibration at 4% for 3 min.

◆ 주요 결과 — 3. Results
  · NTA showed that the particles had a mean diameter of 151.9 nm, indicating that the preparation was enriched in nanoscale vesicles within the operational size range generally assigned to small extracellular vesicles.
  · The total protein concentration of the SDEV preparation was 3.537 mg/mL, confirming the presence of protein-containing material and providing a basis for dose normalization in subsequent experiments.
  · For Ki-67 immunofluorescence staining, cells were incubated with a rabbit monoclonal anti-Ki-67 antibody followed by an Alexa Fluor® 488-conjugated anti-rabbit IgG agment antibody.
  · Ki-67-positive cells in the G1, S, G2, and M phases are shown in green, whereas quiescent G0-phase cells are Ki-67-negative.
  · After 24 h of treatment, the proportion of EdU-positive cells increased from 20.0% to 23.5%, while the proportion of Ki-67-positive cells increased from 68.8% to 70.8%.
  · After 48 h of treatment, the proportion of EdU-positive cells increased from 18.9% to 23.5%, and the proportion of Ki-67-positive cells increased from 73.9% to 80.0%.
  · Azide 594: red, excitation/emission maxima: 590/615 nm, DAPI: blue, excitation/emission maxima: 358/461 nm, Alexa Fluor® 488: excitation/emission maxima: 495/519 nm.

◆ 고찰 — 4. Discussion
  · The present study suggests that spruce-derived extracellular vesicles (SDEVs) may activate dermal papilla cells (DPCs) and accelerate hair regrowth in depilated C57BL/6 mice.
  · Increased EdU incorporation and Ki-67 positivity provide more direct evidence of DPCs proliferation than the CCK-8 assay.
  · In the present animal experiment, more pronounced hair regrowth was observed in the SDEVs group on day 14, suggesting that SDEVs may accelerate the initiation and early progression of hair regeneration.
  · However, by day 21, hair regrowth was nearly complete in both the blank control and SDEV groups.
