제목: Biosome Therapy for Androgenetic Alopecia in a Patient With Incomplete Response to Minoxidil and Finasteride: A Case Report
저널: Clinical case reports
저자: Almashali M 외
발행: 2026 Oct
원문: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42818892/

[한눈에 보기]
- A 25-year-old man with androgenetic alopecia showed limited response to long-term minoxidil and finasteride.
- Three monthly Biosome treatment sessions were associated with progressive clinical improvement in apparent hair coverage on serial follow-up photographs, without treatment-related adverse events during the 3-month observation period.
- This case provides a hypothesis-generating clinical observation supporting further investigation of Biosome-based therapy using standardized and objective outcome measures.
