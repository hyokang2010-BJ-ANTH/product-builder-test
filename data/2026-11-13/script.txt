◆ 배경 — Introduction
  · Alopecia areata (AA) is an autoimmune disease characterized by non-scarring hair loss.
  · Although ritlecitinib has demonstrated efficacy in moderate-to-severe AA, real-world data on 48-week outcomes and predictors of longer-term response remain limited.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · We conducted a retrospective, single-center, observational study of 41 patients with moderate-to-severe AA who received ritlecitinib (50 mg/day) at Osaka Metropolitan University Hospital, Japan.
  · Effectiveness and safety were evaluated for up to 48 weeks.
  · Patients evaluable at week 48 were classified as responders (Severity of Alopecia Tool [SALT] score ≤ 20) or non-responders (SALT score > 20).
  · The predictive value of the change in SALT score from baseline (ΔSALT) for response at week 48 was assessed using receiver operating characteristic (ROC) analysis.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · At week 48, 39.3% (11/28) and 35.7% (10/28) of patients achieved SALT scores ≤ 20 and ≤ 10, respectively.
  · Responders at week 48 (week-48 responders) showed greater improvement in ΔSALT at week 24 (week-24 ΔSALT) than non-responders (median: - 50.0 vs. 0.0; P = 0.006).
  · Of the 21 patients who had not achieved a SALT score ≤ 20 at week 24, five subsequently achieved this outcome at week 48.
  · ROC analysis showed that week-24 ΔSALT had predictive value for week-48 response (area under the curve [AUC] 0.81; 95% confidence interval 0.619-1.000), with a cutoff of - 40 yielding 81.8% sensitivity and 88.2% specificity.
  · Two patients (4.9%) discontinued ritlecitinib because of adverse events; all other adverse events were mild and did not require treatment discontinuation.

◆ 결론 — Conclusions
  · Ritlecitinib showed real-world effectiveness through 48 weeks in patients with moderate-to-severe AA.
  · Delayed responses beyond week 24 were observed, and week-24 ΔSALT may have potential value in predicting response at week 48, although these findings require validation in larger cohorts.
