◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · This essay examines the costs of regulatory failure by highlighting the serious neuropsychiatric risks associated with finasteride, a popular hair-loss medication that can cause long-lasting depression and suicidal thoughts.
  · In the United States, illustrative estimates suggest that hundreds of thousands of users may experience depression, over a thousand may die by suicide, and the associated societal costs may approach several billion dollars annually.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · The article criticizes the FDA and the pharmaceutical industry for failing to conduct or require thorough post-marketing safety studies despite early warning signs.
  · The author concludes that public health must take precedence over corporate interests and shareholder duties.
  · To prevent future similar events, the article proposes regulatory reform that mandates ongoing surveillance.
  · Regulators must require manufacturers to commit to continuous safety monitoring and formal studies after a medication is approved.
  · Regulators must enforce these post-approval study requirements with clear timelines, rather than relying on industry self-discipline, to rebuild public trust and protect citizens from drug safety failures.
