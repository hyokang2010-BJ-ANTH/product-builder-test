◆ 배경 — Background
  · Chemotherapy-induced alopecia (CIA) is common, distressing, and markedly impairs patients' quality of life.
  · Although scalp cooling is the only approved preventive strategy, its effectiveness and accessibility remain limited.
  · Therefore, alternative pharmacologic approaches, such as minoxidil and prostaglandin analogs, have been explored.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · This scoping review aimed to explore the available evidence on minoxidil's efficacy and safety and the potential role of prostaglandin analogs in alopecia.
  · PubMed, Cochrane Library, Medical Online, and Ichushi-Web were comprehensively searched from inception to February 2026.
  · Studies evaluating minoxidil in CIA and prostaglandin analogs in any alopecia type were included.
  · Case reports, case series, and non-human studies were excluded.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · Five studies evaluated minoxidil in CIA, including two randomized controlled trials and three retrospective studies on prophylactic and therapeutic use, respectively.
  · Prophylactic topical minoxidil did not markedly prevent CIA but was associated with earlier hair regrowth.
  · In contrast, therapeutic use-particularly of oral minoxidil-improved hair density and treatment response.
  · No serious adverse events were reported, although cardiovascular effects remain a concern with oral formulations.
  · Two randomized controlled trials evaluated prostaglandin analogs in non-chemotherapy-related alopecia and showed markedly improved hair density and regrowth.
  · However, no study directly assessed their efficacy in CIA.

◆ 결론 — Conclusions
  · Minoxidil may be beneficial for hair regrowth in CIA but shows limited preventive efficacy.
  · Prostaglandin analogs are promising but untested in this setting.
  · Future studies should evaluate their role, particularly in combination with minoxidil, to address the current evidence gap and improve outcomes for patients with CIA.
