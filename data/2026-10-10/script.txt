◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Pseudofolliculitis barbae (PFB) is a chronic inflammatory disorder triggered by shaving and disproportionately affects individuals of African descent.
  · For service members, medically appropriate management may conflict with military grooming standards requiring a clean-shaven appearance.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · Recent policy changes have tightened restrictions on shaving waivers and may create career consequences for service members who still require accommodations despite treatment.
  · These policies raise ethical concerns involving justice, beneficence, nonmaleficence, and autonomy.
  · Military safety requirements related to protective equipment must be balanced against the clinical needs of patients with PFB.
  · Laser hair removal may provide meaningful benefit but can recur, permanently alter facial hair growth, and carry additional risks depending on skin type, device selection, and treatment setting.
  · Dermatologists should provide accurate medical assessments, support medically necessary accommodations, and use shared decision-making so that treatment remains guided by clinical need and patient preference rather than institutional pressure.
