◆ 배경 — Background (자동 구분)
  · Hair loss or alopecia is a universal problem faced by both men and women around the world.
  · An individual's self-esteem is affected by alopecia, and it may lead to psychological stress and anxiety.
  · Topical minoxidil, oral finasteride, and hair transplantation are the common procedures for treating alopecia.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results (자동 구분)
  · But these approaches are associated with various challenges due to high cost, poor patient compliance, and undesired side effects, thereby demanding new strategies for treating alopecia.
  · Identifying novel approaches to treat alopecia has long been a major concern of dermatological research.
  · Recently, microneedles (MNs) have received enormous attention in the field of transdermal drug delivery due to their biocompatible, noninvasive, and painless approach.
  · They are micron-sized needles that have received huge attention in hair loss treatment.
  · MNs can improve the efficiency of drugs as they can puncture the stratum corneum and release the therapeutic molecules at the site of action.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion (자동 구분)
  · This review highlights the potential use of MN-based therapies in treating alopecia.
