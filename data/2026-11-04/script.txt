[상세 내용] (초록 5개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background
  · Advances in follicular unit excision (FUE) have substantially improved procedural efficiency.
  · However, real-world extraction and implantation speeds remain poorly characterized.

◆ 배경 — Objective
  · To describe typical FUE extraction and implantation speeds and identify factors associated with procedural speed variation.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · Operative records of FUE performed by 8 surgeons across 5 clinics over 6 months in 2025 were retrospectively reviewed.
  · Eligible procedures involved single-surgeon scalp-to-scalp FUE exceeding 1,000 grafts.
  · Extraction and implantation speeds were measured during active surgical graft harvesting and placement.
  · Associations between procedural speed and patient, procedural, surgeon, and device-related factors were evaluated using univariate and multiple regression analyses.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · Analyses included 612 FUE sessions across 526 patients.
  · Mean extraction and implantation speeds were 725.7 and 697.6 grafts/h, respectively.
  · Increased extraction speed was independently associated with larger case sizes, surgeon experience ≥1 year, and a dedicated graft-pulling assistant (all p < .001).
  · Increased implantation speed was independently associated with multi-implanter device utilization (p < .001).
  · Extraction speed was significantly reduced during revision cases (p < .001).
  · Patient age and hair characteristics showed no significant association with speed.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion
  · Real-world FUE extraction and implantation speeds approximate 700 grafts/h, providing a practical benchmark for operative planning and clinical counseling.
