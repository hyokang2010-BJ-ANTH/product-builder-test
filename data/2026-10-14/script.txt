[상세 내용] (초록 5개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background
  · Combination therapy with scalp microneedling and topical minoxidil is more effective than minoxidil alone in androgenetic alopecia (AGA), yet treatment responses vary considerably among patients and the multidimensional structure of this heterogeneity remains poorly characterized.

◆ 배경 — Objective
  · To explore whether unsupervised clustering identifies distinct multidimensional treatment-response profiles during combination therapy and to describe their clinical and molecular characteristics as a hypothesis-generating basis for personalized management.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · In this prospective cohort study, 156 patients with AGA received standardized combination therapy (microneedling 1.0 mm depth, biweekly; 5% topical minoxidil, twice daily) for 24 weeks, with longitudinal trichoscopic, molecular and clinical assessments.
  · K-means clustering was applied to ten standardized treatment-response (change-from-baseline) variables; the number of clusters was examined by silhouette, gap-statistic and bootstrap-stability analyses, and between-profile differences were tested with assumption-appropriate methods and reported with effect sizes.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · Silhouette analysis indicated weak global cluster structure (k=2, 0.274; k=3, 0.168); a three-cluster solution was retained as an exploratory, clinically motivated grouping and labelled Robust (n=45, 28.8%), Moderate (n=56, 35.9%) and Limited (n=55, 35.3%) responders.
  · Profiles differed across the response variables that defined them, including hair-density change (36.25±19.07 vs 17.76±10.93 hairs/cm2 for Robust vs Limited; ε2=0.19) and Wnt10b transcript change (0.80±0.37 vs 0.27±0.19; ε2=0.34).
  · Baseline clinical and molecular parameters, including LEF-1 and IGF-1, did not differ across profiles and did not predict profile membership.

◆ 결론 — Conclusion
  · Unsupervised clustering identified three clinically interpretable treatment-response profiles accompanied by graded differences in Wnt/β-catenin-associated transcript changes.
  · Because the profiles showed weak internal separation and could not be predicted from baseline characteristics, these hypothesis-generating findings suggest, rather than demonstrate, multidimensional structure in treatment response and require validation in independent, controlled cohorts before any clinical application.
