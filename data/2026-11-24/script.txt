[상세 내용] (초록 5개 항목 정리 — 이 논문은 전문이 아직 공개되지 않아 초록을 씁니다)

◆ 배경 — Background
  · Photobiomodulation (PBM) uses red and near-infrared (NIR) light to induce non-thermal, photochemical effects through mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase activation.
  · Its evidence base in aesthetic surgery has evolved substantially since the last comprehensive plastic surgery review.

◆ 배경 — Objectives
  · To compare the strength of evidence for PBM in photorejuvenation, body contouring, and hair restoration, and to provide practical recommendations.

◆ 연구 방법 — Methods
  · A narrative review was conducted in accordance with the Scale for the Assessment of Narrative Review Articles.
  · MEDLINE, EMBASE, EBM Reviews, and Google Scholar were searched through March 2026 for clinical studies, systematic reviews, and consensus statements of red (600-700 nm) and NIR (700-1100 nm) PBM.
  · Levels of evidence followed the hierarchy adopted by the 2025 multidisciplinary Delphi consensus on PBM.

◆ 주요 결과 — Results
  · Androgenetic alopecia carries the strongest support (Level IA), with meta-analyses reporting density gains of 17 to 24 hairs/cm2, comparable to finasteride in indirect comparisons and topical minoxidil in head-to-head trials.
  · Photorejuvenation carries moderate support (Level IB), as sham-controlled RCTs using objective endpoints have produced inconsistent results.
  · Evidence for body contouring remains limited, with one systematic review of three RCTs reporting modest circumferential reductions and two negative sham-controlled trials.
  · Adverse events were mild and transient.
  · All domains share small sample sizes, manufacturer-funded trials, heterogeneous protocols, and short follow-up.

◆ 결론 — Conclusions
  · PBM is a safe adjunct within the plastic surgeon's multimodal aesthetic framework, best supported for androgenetic alopecia.
  · Standardized protocols, independent replication, and head-to-head comparisons are needed before broader adoption.
